Inspection Metrics Formulas Reference

Defect Density (DD) DD = Total Defects / Document Size Measured per Page, KLOC, or Function Point.
Defect Removal Efficiency (DRE) DRE = (Resolved / Total) × 100 Percentage of defects removed pre-release.
Closure Rate (CR) CR = (Closed / Total) × 100 Percentage of verified & closed defects.
Average Resolution Time ART = Avg(Resolved_At - Created_At) Average days taken to fix design defects.
